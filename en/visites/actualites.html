Rochefort en Terre

A village full of character, once voted the French people's favourite village, which was historically the administrative centre for the schist quarries of the surrounding villages. The painter Alfred Klots discovered Rochefort en Terre in 1903; he had the present-day château built and helped adorn the village with its now-famous flowers.

The Landes de Cojoux

20 minutes from our guesthouse, Brittany's second-largest megalithic site, set in wild countryside that bursts into colour every spring with gorse and broom.

Watch the video

Île aux Pies

A great spot to relax in nature: this natural site can be reached from the Bains-sur-Oust side (35) or the Saint-Vincent-sur-Oust side (56), and even by canoe from Glénac. The Saint-Vincent bank is a towpath along the Oust; the Bains-sur-Oust bank is wilder, with more facilities (including climbing).

Watch the video

Peuples en Images

The association "Peuples en Images" ("Peoples in Pictures") celebrates the customs, rites and culture of peoples around the world. Since 1991, Didier Haslé has filmed numerous documentaries in Brittany around Redon, as well as in Ecuador and Romania (the village of Lăpuș, in the Carpathians).

Jazz Redon

Since 2014, a jazz festival has been held in Redon and the surrounding area for a week, generally from 9 to 15 December.

Festival website

Lizio arts & crafts fair

Every year around 10 August, the village of Lizio holds an arts and crafts fair. Around 80 craftspeople showcase their skills, with food on site and Breton music concerts in the streets.

The rest of the year, around Les Fougerêts

Rochefort en Terre at Christmas

Throughout December, the village of Rochefort en Terre lights up its streets and has, over the years, become a must-see for the festive season — it was voted the French people's favourite village in 2016. Enquire at the town hall: +33 2 97 43 32 81.

Le Pont du Rock

A major rock festival in Malestroit, at the Daufresne site, gathering several thousand festival-goers every year. Since 2018, it has been held on the first weekend of August.

Mots-zyk sous les pins

Every November, the village of Saint-Jacut-les-Pins (56) hosts a rock festival with an eclectic line-up. Information: +33 6 85 56 63 22.

Autumn fruit festival — Peillac

Every year in mid-October, this festival celebrates walking, sung rambles, music and local crafts.

Les Vendredis du canal — Malestroit

A free, not-to-be-missed weekly music evening: every Friday from 9:30 pm, from the last weekend of June to the last weekend of August, at Place du Bouffay, with artists from a wide range of genres — perfect for a family evening out on the terrace.

La Pomme d'Orange — Peillac

Concerts, shows and markets in November and December in this small Morbihan village, brightening the "dark months" before the winter solstice. Information: +33 2 99 91 25 26.

La Bogue d'Or — Redon

In late October, La Bogue d'Or is the great festival of Gallo singing in Redon: performances, traditional boat crews and a cultural programme across the whole Redon area, organised by the Groupement culturel breton.

Glénac motocross

Every April, a motocross race on a 3.5-hectare, 1.6 km track — a popular event with enthusiasts.

Mountain bike downhill — Les Fougerêts

In June, a well-known mountain bike downhill event, one of the village's flagship activities.

Sea shanties — Les Fougerêts

Early August, at the Les Fougerêts municipal stadium from 7 pm: an evening with the group Les Boucaniers (from the Saint-Malo area), followed by a Breton fest-noz dance on a wooden floor. Mussels and chips, dessert and coffee for €12 (reduced rate for children). Contact: L'Espoir de l'Oust.

La Gacilly photo festival

Every year in early June, more than 1,000 photographs and around forty exhibitors take over the streets, lanes, gardens and park of La Gacilly. In 2019, the 16th edition celebrated Russia and Eastern Europe.

Saint Jacob Guesthouse

An 18th-century hunting lodge in Les Fougerêts, 4 km from La Gacilly, southern Brittany.
